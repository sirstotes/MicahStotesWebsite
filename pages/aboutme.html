Get to Know Me

Hey there,

I go by Micah Stotesbery, I'm an actor based in Salt Lake City, Utah, and my favorite animal is a raccoon even though I've only ever seen a real one from 20 yards away on a dark bike path at 11pm. When it comes to performance, I'd say I excel at the highly technical aspects: unique accents, tight harmonies, complicated memorization, etc. I always feel the most myself when I am challenged, and acting is no exception.

I started performing as a kid, playing music with my father and brothers. I started getting involved in theatre during high school and when I went to college I decided it was the thing that excited me most. I have recently graduated with my BFA in Acting Performance from Utah Valley University. I mostly have experience with live theatre, both plays and musicals, but recently I have begun acting in film as well. In my roles, I try to bring realism, authenticity, and parts of myself to the character, which usually means I accidentally imbue them with queer subtext. I often play the outsider character: someone who doesn't quite fit in or is forced into the antagonist role. Examples of this from my recent work include Professor Bhaer in Little Women and Ben Weatherstaff from The Secret Garden. But I believe that any villain in a story has layers you can peel back to reveal a character full of depth and sympathy.

My whole life, I have struggled with connecting with and befriending my peers. I always try to be kind, but I'm not the most sociable person. I always found online interactions to be easier than in-person ones. This changed when I started performing, and I'm very interested in how performing arts can empower individuals and spark connection between people of different backgrounds. As a hobbyist computer programmer and game developer, my dream is to bring live performance to lonely people through technology and the internet, using the mediums of film and virtual reality.

Beyond acting, I spend my time working on various programming projects (I programmed this website from scratch!), drawing, crocheting, or playing indie games. Acting is one of my many creative outlets, and I am always creating things in various mediums. If any of this interests you, consider subscribing to my YouTube channel to stay updated with my work and projects.
