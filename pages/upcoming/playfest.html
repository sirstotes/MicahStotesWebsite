Playfest In The Park

I'm participating in a play festival this August, playing roles in three seperate new works by local writers.

Missing You
Written and directed by Conner Kaiponui Webb
My role: Dylan Howard
Synopsis:
Chaos ensues when River comes to a funeral hosted by the Howards, uninvited. There, they are met with their ex, Dylan Howard, who actively tries to get them to leave. This all leads to a whirlwind of emotions and memories flooding back, until it becomes hard to not miss what they used to be to each other. As family gets involved and opinions become too heavy, the funeral ends in shambles and we forget where we were in the first place: at a funeral.

I Need a Hero
Written by Anne Magee, directed by Abbie Cochrane
My role: Friend
Synopsis:
In the middle of a particularly hopeless battle, the Hero is miraculously transported into the world of the writer who tells their story, only to discover that not even the writer knows how to get them out of this one. As the writer works through their writer's block, they and the Hero find themselves trying to answer the same question: What do we do when we're fight a battle we can't win?

The Pirate Lord: In Concert
Written and directed by Kira Wootton
My role: Calico Jack, the Pirate Lord
Synopsis:
A new musical based on the true story of Anne Bonny and Mary Reed: A female pirate and female british soldier, both disguised as men, who both fall in love at a time where you would least expect it - a pirate ship in the 1720s.

Performance Dates: Aug 14th & 15th
